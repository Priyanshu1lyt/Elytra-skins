# Elytra Skins

Client-side Fabric mod for **Minecraft Java 1.21.11** by **NotHeartYT**.
Gives you **208 Elytra skins** in 16 categories, a neon GUI with search, favorites, recents, a rotating preview, and a favorites randomizer.

## Use
- Press **K** in-game to open the GUI (rebind in Controls > Elytra Skins).
- Pick a skin, check the rotating preview, press **APPLY**. **REMOVE** restores the vanilla Elytra.
- **FAV RANDOMIZER: ON** picks a random favorite every time you equip an Elytra.
- Settings are saved to `config/elytraskins.json`.

## Categories (13 skins each)
Anime, Dragon, Demon, Angel, Fire, Ice, Galaxy, Nether, Ender, RGB, Neon, Crystal, Nature, Gaming, Legendary, Special Edition.

## Client-only
Nothing is sent to the server. Servers and other players do not need the mod. Only your own screen changes; gameplay is untouched.

## Skins are generated, not stored as PNGs
Each skin is a 64x32 texture generated in code (`SkinGenerator.java`) from its own pattern, palette and seed, then cached (small LRU) and uploaded only when applied.

## Build
- GitHub: push, then open **Actions > build** and download the `ElytraSkins` artifact.
- Local: JDK 21 and Gradle 9.2, then `gradle build`. The jar is in `build/libs/`.
- The mod icon is generated by the `generateIcon` Gradle task during the build.

## Known limits
- The skin replaces the vanilla Elytra texture on your client, so other players' plain Elytras look the same on your screen. Players wearing a cape keep the cape texture on their Elytra (vanilla behavior).
- Sodium/Iris: no mixins or custom render code, so it should be compatible.
